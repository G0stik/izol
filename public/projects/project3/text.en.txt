Another fantastic reference is undoubtedly the tallest residential building in Bratislava, #KLINGERKA (JTRE), which stands on the site of a former textile factory producing jute. Wallpaper, leatherette, sacks, etc. were made here...

A few basic facts:
115 meters is the height of the building
35 above-ground floors
380 two- to four-room apartments
376 balconies
5 elevators
663 stairs from the garage to the 35th floor
678 parking spaces
425 m2 of retail area
6,300 m2 of greenery (park + green roof of the garage house)
