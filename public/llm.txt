## Products and solutions

### K-FLEX synthetic rubber insulation

Izol system is the official Slovak importer and distributor of K-FLEX insulation. Typical applications include cold water and chilled water distribution, heating, air-conditioning, HVAC ductwork, ventilation systems, and solar distribution systems. Highlighted product systems include:

- K-FLEX H-DUCT METAL for HVAC duct insulation.
- K-FLEX AL CLAD with UV-stable aluminum surface, used as an alternative to sheet-metal cladding outdoors.
- K-FLEX TWIN SOLAR for solar collector circuits.
- K-FLEX K-FONIK acoustic and technical insulation products where relevant.

### Refractory and high-temperature insulation

The company supplies UNIFRAX / SIBRAL refractory insulation based on ceramic/silica fibers for high-temperature applications up to +1430 C. Typical use cases:

- flues, chimneys, furnace linings, boiler insulation, burners, ovens, heating chambers,
- metallurgy, foundry, automotive, steel, and other industrial environments,
- pipe systems and equipment exposed to very high temperatures.

### Building insulation

The range includes EPS and XPS polystyrene, mineral wool, and glass wool products from leading manufacturers including ROCKWOOL, KNAUF INSULATION, and ISOVER. Applications:

- pitched roofs and flat roofs,
- partitions and floors,
- contact and ventilated facades,
- residential, commercial, and industrial construction.

### Technical and fire-protection insulation

Izol system supplies technical insulation for pipework, steam lines, tanks, chimneys, HVAC ducts, and fire-protection systems. Fire-resistance systems mentioned on the website include EI30, EI45, EI60, EI90, EI120, and EI240 solutions:

- ORSTECH H, ORSTECH LSP PYRO,
- PYROROCK and FIRESTOP systems,
- CONLIT DUCTROCK,
- U PROTECT SLAB,
- PROMAT,
- ISOVER CLIMCOVER and related HVAC insulation.

### Polyethylene insulation and accessories

The company also supplies TUBEX and MIRELON polyethylene insulation for heating, plumbing, packaging, and expansion-joint applications. Accessories include aluminum tapes, PE tapes, rubber tapes, UV-stable tapes, adhesives, cleaners, welding pins, welding guns, and small tools.
